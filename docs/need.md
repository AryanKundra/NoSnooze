The Need For Our App:

THE PROBLEM: Passive Morning Behavior/Sleep Inertia

Most people who use a smartphone typically begin their day with an alarm going off. A sizeable number of time may tap snooze/dismiss it and either go back to bed or start scrolling on social media before getting out of bed. This habit has two outstanding problems.

1. Morning Dommscrollings Impact on Mental Health:
Research and commentary surrounding public health have documented the impact that checkign social media first thing in the morniing has. Short-form content (such as TikTok,Instagram reels, YT shorts) is engineered in such a way as to explit the dopamine reward circutry and engaging in the stragiht in the morning is espically damaging as the brain is in a transitonal state as it moves from sleep to being awake. Keilyn Goatley (2025) writing for Still Waters Therapy describes how scrolling in the morning create an immediate dopamine spike which is followed shorly by a crash, this contrutes to brain fog, stress, aswell as reduced conginive clarity for the rest of the day. Starting your morning with a more mindful/active task have reported to stay more clear headed throught the day and espically in the transition period of sleep to wake. 

"Why Your Morning Scroll is Wrecking Your Mental Health (and What to Do Instead)" — Keilyn Goatley, 2025
https://www.stillwaterstherapy.org/post/morning-scroll-mental-health


2. Sleep Inertia:
sleep inertia is defined as a transitonal state of reduced arousal and impaired congitie and behavioral performance immediately upon waking up. Ogawa et al. (2022) released a study which measured the effects of pressing snooze on the alarm and found that those who repleatedly delayed the alarm worsen their sleep inertia which leads to increasing fatiuge and cognitive impairment in the first few hours of the day. The snooze habit is so normal that most users expeirnece its adverse effects without realizing it. 
"Snooze alarm use in a global population of smartphone users" — Ogawa et al., 2022
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9804954/#Sec21



OUR APPROACH:
NoSnooze addresses both problems by replacing the one-tap dismiss screen for the alarm with a short puzzle ranging from mental arthimetic to a triva question, or a number patter. The app introduces a short cognitive activity upon waking which serves two purposes:

1. reducing sleep inertia
by the time the user has read/answered the puzzle their brain has been forced into a level of active processing which disrupts the half-asleep state that snoozing prolongs
2. Breaking the habit of scrolling:
now of course this doesnt stop you from scrolling after you solve the puzzle but for those who reflexive go to their social media platform of choice its our hope that solving a quick puzzle might help to break that habit and get your day started with something more constructive.

The puzzles are simple and accessible and arent meant to be challenging in a stressful way, they are designed to be just engaging enough to help the brain out of sleep inertia and if you are unable to solve the problem (or too lazy i suppose) you can shake your phone inorder to see the answer. Users also have the option to choose 1 out of three puzzle types which were already mentions: math, triva, pattern.

- anyone looking to be more active and have a more intetentional start to their day.
